Rush is a Material 3 lyrics app for Android that finds, syncs, saves and shares the lyrics of the songs you love.

Features

- Instant lyrics search for any song, by title or artist
- Line-synced and syllable-synced lyrics that highlight word by word as the song plays
- Romanized lyrics for Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Punjabi and other Cyrillic languages
- Rush Mode: detects the song playing on your device and switches the lyrics automatically
- Save lyrics for offline viewing, sort them, and back up or export your library
- Fix wrong or missing lyrics with the built-in lyrics correction tool
- Share lyrics as a customizable card: 9 styles, custom colors, corner shapes and album art shapes
- Full-screen share cards in vertical, couplet, quote, messy and brat layouts
- Highly customizable lyrics view: font, size, line height, letter spacing and alignment
- Animated and audio-reactive backgrounds that react to the music: wave, gradient, curve and hypnotic
- Material 3 Expressive design with wallpaper-based theming, AMOLED dark mode and app font selection
- Built for phones, tablets, foldables and desktops with an adaptive, large-screen ready layout
- No ads, no tracking, open source under GPL-3.0, and a FOSS build with every feature unlocked
- Available in 30+ languages

Lyrics are provided by LRCLIB and Genius, and synced timing comes from the community.

Rush is free and open source. Support the development or translate the app on GitHub.
